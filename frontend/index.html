Questions, answered

Everything you need to know.

Is my data secure?

Access is role-based and your team can export operational data. Use strong passwords and limit access to the people who need it.

Are backups included?

Professional and Enterprise plans include backup planning and export workflows. Ask us about retention options for your setup.

Can I migrate from Excel?

Yes. Start with a product or customer CSV and we can help you map the columns during setup.

Does it work offline?

Scanimart is designed for a connected browser session. For reliable stock syncing, keep the billing device online.

Is there a mobile app?

The responsive web app works on mobile browsers today. A dedicated mobile app is on the roadmap.

Can I change plans later?

Yes. Start with Starter and contact us when your team needs more users, automation or support.
